PAYMENT PROCESSING PLAN

For custom jobs, quote-first payment is safer than charging before specifications are reviewed.

Needed to add payments:
- A merchant account with a payment provider (Stripe or Square are common choices).
- GingerSnap business/contact/bank details required by that provider.
- Decide deposit policy: full payment, percentage deposit, or invoice balance.
- Decide which products have fixed prices and which remain quote-only.
- Hosted HTTPS deployment.
- Backend endpoints for creating checkout sessions/payment links.
- A verified webhook endpoint to record successful/failed/refunded payments.
- Order statuses such as NEW → QUOTED → APPROVED → PAID → IN PRODUCTION → READY/SHIPPED.
- Tax/shipping rules appropriate to the business.

Never store raw credit/debit card numbers on the GingerSnap server.
